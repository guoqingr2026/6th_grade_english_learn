# Daily Phrases

Here are some useful phrases you can use in everyday situations.

## Introducing Yourself
* **"Hello, my name is [Name]."**
* **"Nice to meet you."**
* **"I am from [Country/City]."**
* **"I am learning English."**

## Asking for Clarification
If you don't understand something in English, you can use these polite phrases:
* **"Could you repeat that, please?"** 
* **"Could you speak a little slower, please?"**
* **"What does [word] mean?"**
* **"How do you spell that?"**
* **"I don't understand."**

## Shopping and Ordering
* **"How much does this cost?"**
* **"I would like to order [food item], please."**
* **"Can I get the bill/check, please?"**
* **"Do you accept credit cards?"**

## Asking for Directions
* **"Excuse me, where is the nearest restroom / toilet?"**
* **"How do I get to the train station?"**
* **"Is it far from here?"**

---
*Practice Task: Try reading these out loud to practice your pronunciation!*
