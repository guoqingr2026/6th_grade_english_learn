第六步：全新知识点逐一精讲

🔑 知识点1：肯定句结构

Subject + Verb (past form)

📖 【新知识讲解】

一般过去时肯定句：主语 + 动词过去式 + 其他。时间标志词：last weekend, yesterday, over the summer holidays。

结构例句中文
规则 -edI visited the museum.我参观了博物馆。
不规则She went to Xi'an.她去了西安。
be 动词It was amazing!太棒了！

🏰 【记忆宫殿法】

时间轴宫殿：入口牌 last weekend → 走廊 climbed/visited → 终点柜 went/saw/ate。沿时间轴走一遍，肯定句全会！

✍️ 【举一反三练习】

写出过去式：
go→___  see→___  eat→___  climb→___  visit→___

造肯定句（各 1 句）：
1. last Sunday + go
2. yesterday + visit
3. last year + climb

🔑 知识点2：否定句 didn't

I didn't go to Beijing.

📖 【新知识讲解】

否定句用 didn't（did not）+ 动词原形，过去式变化全部「还原」成原形！

肯定否定中文
I went to Beijing.I didn't go to Beijing.我没去北京。
She climbed the wall.She didn't climb the wall.她没爬长城。
They visited Paris.They didn't visit Paris.他们没参观巴黎。

🎵 【歌曲记忆法】

（曲调：《上学歌》）Didn't didn't，否定来；动词原形放后面；Went climbed visited，全部变回原形！

✍️ 【举一反三练习】

否定句转换：
1. I went to Shanghai. → I ___ go to Shanghai.
2. She visited the museum. → She ___ visit the museum.
3. They climbed the hill. → They ___ climb the hill.

答案：1.didn't  2.didn't  3.didn't

🔑 知识点3：疑问句 Did...?

Did you climb the Great Wall? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

📖 【新知识讲解】

一般疑问句：Did + 主语 + 动词原形? 肯定答 Yes, 主语 + did. 否定答 No, 主语 + didn't.

问句肯定答否定答
Did you climb the Great Wall?Yes, I did.No, I didn't.
Did she go to Xi'an?Yes, she did.No, she didn't.
Did they take photos?Yes, they did.No, they didn't.

🏰 【记忆宫殿法】

电话亭宫殿：拿起听筒问 Did you...?；按绿色键答 Yes, I did；按红色键答 No, I didn't。三色按钮，疑问应答分清！

✍️ 【举一反三练习】

句型转换：
1. You visited the museum. → ___ you ___ the museum?
2. She went to Paris. → ___ she ___ to Paris?
3. They climbed the hill. → ___ they ___ the hill?

问答配对：自编 3 组 Did 问句与答句。

🔑 知识点4：不规则动词速记

go→went see→saw eat→ate take→took

📖 【新知识讲解】

不规则动词没有 -ed 规律，需要整词记忆。本单元高频：go→went, see→saw, eat→ate, take→took, send→sent。

原形过去式例句
gowentI went to Xi'an.
seesawWe saw the warriors.
eatateThey ate noodles.
taketookI took many photos.

🎵 【歌曲记忆法】

（口诀歌）Go went走，See saw看，Eat ate吃，Take took拍；Send sent发，五个兄弟记心间！

✍️ 【举一反三练习】

不规则动词填空：
1. I ___ (go) to Beijing last year.
2. We ___ (see) the Terracotta Warriors.
3. She ___ (eat) Xi'an noodles.
4. They ___ (take) many photos.

答案：1.went  2.saw  3.ate  4.took

课文填空与问答精讲

  1. I ___ the Great Wall. (climb)
    climbed — 规则动词过去式 + -ed。
  2. Did you ___ to Beijing? (go)
    go — Did 后接动词原形。
  3. Where ___ you go? (do)
    did — 特殊疑问句 Did 提前。
  4. We ___ bikes on the wall. (ride)
    rode — ride 不规则过去式。
  5. 翻译：我上周末爬了长城。
    I climbed the Great Wall last weekend.
  6. 规则 vs 不规则：visit / go / eat 过去式
    visited / went / ate

语法巩固仿写 · 一般过去时日记

Last weekend, I went to Beijing with my parents. We climbed the Great Wall and visited the Palace Museum. We took many photos. It was an amazing trip!

得分要点：时间状语 + went/climbed/visited + 感受句。

第七步：综合复习

  1. 「句型三轮变」：同一句话轮流变肯定、否定、疑问。
  2. 「不规则动词接龙」：说原形，下一位说过去式。
  3. 语法闯关：10 道填空，正确率 80% 过关。
