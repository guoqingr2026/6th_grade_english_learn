第六步：全新知识点逐一精讲

🔑 知识点1：Did you...? 一般疑问句

Did you eat mooncakes? — Yes, we did. / No, we didn't.

📖 【新知识讲解】

询问过去是否做了某事，用 Did + 主语 + 动词原形? 这是 Unit 2 最核心的全新语法！

问句肯定答否定答
Did you eat mooncakes?Yes, we did.No, we didn't.
Did you clean the house?Yes, I did.No, I didn't.
Did you watch the gala?Yes, we did.No, we didn't.

🏰 【记忆宫殿法】

春节红包宫殿：红包正面写 Did you...?；打开见 Yes, I did；空红包写 No, I didn't。一问一答，红包里藏语法！

✍️ 【举一反三练习】

问答练习：
1. Did you ___ (eat) mooncakes? — Yes, we ___.
2. Did she ___ (clean) the house? — No, she ___.
3. ___ you dress in red? — Yes, I ___.

注意：Did 后面用原形 eat，不是 ate！

🔑 知识点2：节日词汇与文化

Spring Festival / Mid-Autumn Festival / mooncakes

📖 【新知识讲解】

中外节日名称首字母大写：the Spring Festival, the Mid-Autumn Festival。节日食物是不可数或复数：mooncakes, zongzi, jiaozi。

🔑 知识点3：春节习俗短语

paste fu on the door / dress in red / count down to the new year

📖 【新知识讲解】

春节三大习俗动词都是规则过去式：paste→pasted, dress→dressed, count→counted。描述习俗用 We pasted fu on the door.

短语过去式句子中文
paste fu on the doorWe pasted fu on the door.我们在门上贴了福字。
dress in redWe dressed in red.我们穿了红色衣服。
count down to the new yearWe counted down to the new year.我们倒计时迎接新年。

🏰 【记忆宫殿法】

春节客厅宫殿：大门贴 pasted fu；镜子前 dressed in red；电视前 counted down——三个位置，习俗全记住！

✍️ 【举一反三练习】

用过去时描述春节：
1. We ___ (clean) the house.
2. We ___ (paste) fu on the door.
3. We ___ (dress) in red.
4. We ___ (count) down to the new year.

答案：1.cleaned  2.pasted  3.dressed  4.counted

🔑 知识点4：How was your holiday?

How was your Mid-Autumn Festival holiday?

📖 【新知识讲解】

节日问候延续 Unit 1 的 How was...? 句型，询问整个假期感受：How was your Mid-Autumn Festival holiday?

问句答句中文
How was your holiday?It was exciting!你假期过得怎么样？— 很激动！
What did you do?We enjoyed mooncakes.你们做了什么？— 我们吃了月饼。
Did you eat mooncakes?Yes, we did.你们吃月饼了吗？— 是的。

🏰 【记忆宫殿法】

节日圆桌宫殿：圆桌中央 How was your holiday?；每人面前一道菜对应一个节日活动，边吃边练英语！

✍️ 【举一反三练习】

综合对话（4 句以上）：
A: How was your Spring Festival?
B: It was great!
A: What did you do?
B: We cleaned the house and pasted fu.
A: Did you eat jiaozi?
B: Yes, we did!
