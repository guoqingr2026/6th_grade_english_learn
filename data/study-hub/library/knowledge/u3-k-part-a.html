课文填空与问答精讲

  1. — How do you feel?
    — I feel ill. My head hurts and I have a runny nose.
  2. Maybe you should see a doctor. (T/F: 表示强烈命令)
    False — should 表建议「应该」，语气较委婉。
  3. You ___ (should/shouldn't) stay up late.
    shouldn't — 熬夜有害健康。
  4. My mum is going to take me to hospital this afternoon.
    be going to 表打算/计划。

课文填空与问答 · 症状描述（满分范例）

任务：用 How do you feel? / should 写 4 句对话。

— How do you feel?
— I feel ill. I have a fever and a cough.
— You should see a doctor and drink more water.
— Thank you. I will get well soon.

得分要点：症状词汇 + should 建议 + 礼貌回应。
