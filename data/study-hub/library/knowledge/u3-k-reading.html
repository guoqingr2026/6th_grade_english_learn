should / shouldn't & be going to

📖 【新知识讲解】

本课核心语法：should / shouldn't & be going to。请对照课文例句理解用法。

英文例句中文
You should exercise.你应该锻炼。
You shouldn't stay up late.你不应该熬夜。
I am going to see a doctor.我打算去看医生。
My head hurts.我头痛。（一般现在时）

✍️ 【举一反三练习】

仿写一段关于 健康生活 的短文（6–8 句）。

课文填空与问答精讲

  1. If you feel ill, you should see a doctor.
    症状 + should 建议句型。
  2. Think about happy things and join clubs.
    心理健康建议：想开心事、参加社团。
  3. What should we do to keep healthy? (至少 3 点)
    healthy diet / exercise / sleep / see a doctor

重要写作练习讲解 · 健康生活短文（满分范文）

How to Keep Healthy

Health is very important. You should eat healthy food and exercise often. You shouldn't stay up late. If you feel ill, you should see a doctor. Think about happy things and join clubs. Let's live a healthy life together!

得分要点：主题句 + 3–4 条 should 建议 + 结尾号召。
