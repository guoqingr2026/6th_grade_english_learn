📖 【新知识讲解】

本课核心语法：be going to & want/need to do。请对照课文例句理解用法。

✍️ 【举一反三练习】

课文填空与问答精讲

  1. — How are you going to spend your pocket money?
    — I am going to buy a schoolbag.
  2. We use money to buy goods and pay for services.
    goods=商品；services=服务。
  3. I am going to buy ___ (a schoolbag).
    be going to + 动词原形。

交际写作 · 零花钱计划对话

— How are you going to spend your pocket money?
— I am going to buy a schoolbag and some books.
— That's a good plan. Don't forget to save some!

得分要点：be going to + 名词；礼貌建议。
