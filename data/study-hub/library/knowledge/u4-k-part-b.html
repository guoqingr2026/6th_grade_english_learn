🔑 知识点1：be going to & want/need to do

be going to & want/need to do

📖 【新知识讲解】

本课核心语法：be going to & want/need to do。请对照课文例句理解用法。

英文例句中文
How are you going to spend it?你打算怎么花？
I am going to buy a schoolbag.我打算买书包。
I want to buy some books.我想买书。

🎵 【歌曲记忆法】

be going to + 动词原形 = 打算；want to do = 想要做

课文填空与问答精讲

  1. — I need to save more.
    need to do = 需要做某事。
  2. — Maybe you can wait for a sale.
    wait for a sale = 等打折再买。
  3. Mike is going to save up for a computer.
    save up for = 攒钱买……
  4. Should I also buy some ice cream? (T/F)
    True — 可用 should 征求意见。

重要写作练习讲解 · 消费计划（满分范文）

写作任务：Write a short paragraph about your spending plan

My Spending Plan

I get 50 yuan of pocket money every month. I am going to save half in my piggy bank. I want to buy a dictionary, but I need to wait for a sale. I shouldn't buy snacks every day. I will use my money wisely!

得分要点：金额 + save / want / need + shouldn't + 总结句。
