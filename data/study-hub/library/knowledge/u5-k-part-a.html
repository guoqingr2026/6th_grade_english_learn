🔑 知识点1：There be & can / can't & 一般过去时

There be & can / can't & 一般过去时

📖 【新知识讲解】

本课核心语法：There be & can / can't & 一般过去时。请对照课文例句理解用法。

英文例句中文
There is a big rocket.有一个大火箭。
There are many stars.有许多星星。
We can use telescopes.我们能用望远镜。
China sent a rover to Mars.中国发送了火星探测器。

🎵 【歌曲记忆法】

There is + 单数 / There are + 复数；can + 动词原形 表示能力

✍️ 【举一反三练习】

仿照课文编一段 4 句对话。

课文填空与问答精讲

  1. — How can we explore space?
    — We can use telescopes.
  2. There are eight planets in our solar system.
    There are + 复数名词。
  3. There ___ (is/are) a big rocket in the museum.
    is — 单数 rocket 用 There is。

描述写作 · 太空博物馆见闻

There is a big rocket in the science museum. There are many photos of planets. We can use telescopes to look into space. Space is amazing!

得分要点：There is/are + can + 感叹句。
