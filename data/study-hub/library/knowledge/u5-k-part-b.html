✍️ 【举一反三练习】

仿写一段关于 探索太空 的短文（6–8 句）。

课文填空与问答精讲

  1. — Can we live on Mars?
    — No, we can't. / Not yet.
  2. Mars is called the "red planet".
    be called = 被称为。
  3. China sent a rover to Mars last year.
    send→sent 不规则过去式。
  4. — Did you see the pictures of Mars?
    — Yes, I did.

重要写作练习讲解 · 火星探索（满分范文）

Mars — The Red Planet

Mars is called the "red planet". Scientists sent a rover to Mars last year. We can't live on Mars yet, but we can learn a lot from the photos. China made us proud. I want to be an astronaut one day!

得分要点：过去式 sent + can/can't + 感受结尾。
