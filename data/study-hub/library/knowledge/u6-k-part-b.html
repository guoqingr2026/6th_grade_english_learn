课文填空与问答精讲

  1. We should save energy every day.
    should 提环保建议。
  2. To protect the Earth, we should reduce, reuse and recycle.
    3R 原则英文。
  3. Take quick showers to save water.
    不定式表目的 to save water。
  4. 3R 代表什么？
    Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

重要写作练习讲解 · 环保建议（满分范文）

Save Energy, Save the Earth

We should save energy every day. We should turn off lights when we leave. We should unplug computers and take quick showers. To protect the Earth, we should reduce, reuse and recycle. Let's start today!

得分要点：多条 should + 3R + 号召句。
