🎵 Unit 1

第一单元 🎵 Travelling around 环游旅行

I visited Beijing,

我去了北京， And I learnt about the place. 我了解了这个地方。 I went to Tian'anmen Square, 我去了天安门广场， And to the Great Wall the next day. 第二天去了长城。

I visited Beijing,

我去了北京， And I learnt about its history. 我了解了它的历史。 I took so many pictures, 我拍了很多照片， And ate new snacks on the street. 在街上吃了新的小吃。

I went to Xi'an next,

接着我去了西安， And I saw the old city wall. 我看到了古老的城墙。 I ate some really tasty noodles, 我吃了一些非常美味的面条， And sang a local song. 还唱了一首当地的歌。

On my trip to Xi'an,

在我的西安之旅中， I saw the famous sights. 我看到了著名的景点。 And climbed up Huashan Mountain. 还爬上了华山。 The views were very nice. 景色非常美丽。

🎵 Unit 2

第二单元 🎵 Getting together 相聚在一起

My family and I, we have a great time.

我和我的家人度过了美好的时光。 We get together at special events. 我们在特殊的节日里相聚。

Events are fun. I love every one.

活动很有趣。我喜欢每一个。 Yeah, yeah, yeah! Yeah, yeah, yeah! 耶，耶，耶！耶，耶，耶！

My parents and I, we had a sports day.

我和我的父母参加了运动会。 There were many fun activities to do. 有很多有趣的活动可以参加。 My brother clapped. 我的哥哥鼓掌喝彩。 Go, go, go! Go, go, go! 加油，加油，加油！加油，加油，加油！

My mother and I, we cleaned up at New Year.

我和我的妈妈在新年打扫卫生。 And pasted fu on our door. 还在我们的门上贴了"福"字。 We dressed in red. All were fed. 我们穿上红色衣服。大家都吃饱了。 Yum, yum, yum! Yum, yum, yum! 真好吃，真好吃，真好吃！

My brother and I, we sang some songs,

我和我的兄弟唱了一些歌， And spoke with all of our relatives. 和所有的亲戚交谈。 We counted down, such a big sound. 我们倒计时，声音好响亮。 3, 2, 1! Fun, fun, fun! 3，2，1！真开心，真开心，真开心！

🎵 Unit 3

第三单元 🎵 How can we stay healthy? 我们怎样保持健康？

How can we stay healthy?

我们怎样保持健康？ The boy has a runny nose. 这个男孩在流鼻涕。 He should see a doctor. 他应该去看医生。 Maybe he knows. 也许他知道。

How can we stay healthy?

我们怎样保持健康？ The girl has a little cough. 这个女孩有点咳嗽。 She should get enough sleep. 她应该保证充足的睡眠。 Let's switch all the lights off. 让我们把所有的灯都关掉。

How can we stay healthy?

我们怎样保持健康？ We shouldn't feel so sad. 我们不应该感到太悲伤。 We should talk about our feelings. 我们应该谈谈我们的感受。 Sharing isn't bad. 分享不是坏事。

How can we stay healthy?

我们怎样保持健康？ Don't feel lonely or alone. 不要感到孤独或孤单。 Join after-school teams and clubs. 参加课外团队和俱乐部。 Have fun at school and at home. 在学校和家里都要快乐。
