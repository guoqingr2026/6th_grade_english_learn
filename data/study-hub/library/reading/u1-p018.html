示例剪贴簿内容（杭州+绍兴之旅）：

Hangzhou

杭州

🗓️ Day 1 We went boating and took nice photos.

我们去划船，拍了很多漂亮的照片。

We walked to the West Lake.

我们步行去了西湖。

🗓️ Day 2 We went to a famous restaurant.

我们去了一家著名的餐厅。

We drank Longjing tea, ate Dongpo pork and Jiao Hua Ji.

我们喝了龙井茶，吃了东坡肉和叫化鸡。

We climbed Baochu Hill and watched the sunrise.

我们爬了宝叔山，观看了日出。

We saw lots of fish in Fish Viewing at the Flower Pond.

我们在花港观鱼观赏了许多鱼。

Shaoxing

绍兴

🗓️ Day 3 We visited the former home of Lu Xun and the Herb Garden.

我们参观了鲁迅故居和百草园。

Lu Xun was a famous writer. He wrote a lot of great books.

鲁迅是一位著名的作家，他写了很多伟大的著作。

✅ 参考答案示例

Share your scrapbook

示例：

I had great fun with my family last summer holidays. We visited Hangzhou and Shaoxing. On Day 1, we went boating on the West Lake and took many nice photos. On Day 2, we climbed Baochu Hill and watched the sunrise. It was amazing! We also ate delicious Dongpo pork and drank Longjing tea. On Day 3, we went to Shaoxing and visited the former home of Lu Xun. We learnt a lot about his life and great books. It was such a wonderful trip!

上个暑假我和家人玩得非常开心。我们参观了杭州和绍兴。第一天，我们在西湖上划船，拍了很多漂亮的照片。第二天，我们爬了宝叔山，观看了日出，真是太壮观了！我们还吃了美味的东坡肉，喝了龙井茶。第三天，我们去了绍兴，参观了鲁迅故居，了解了他的生平和伟大著作。这真是一次美好的旅行！
