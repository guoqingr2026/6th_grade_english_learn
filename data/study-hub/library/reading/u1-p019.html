🟠 Eye-opening findings in Sanxingdui

🟠 三星堆令人大开眼界的发现

My last trip to Sanxingdui was great! In the Sanxingdui Museum, I saw strange masks, the "big guy" statue and a life-size "fruit" tree lamp.

我上次去三星堆的旅行真是太棒了！在三星堆博物馆里，我看到了奇异的面具、"大立人"雕像和一棵真实大小的"果树"灯。

Some believed people from Sanxingdui were aliens. But it's not true. They might eat hotpot three thousand years ago, just like we do today.

有些人认为三星堆的人是外星人，但这并不是真的。他们可能在三千年前就已经吃火锅了，就像我们今天一样。

(Chen Li, history teacher)

（陈丽，历史老师）

🟠 The pearl of Hangzhou

🟠 杭州的明珠

I visited the West Lake last autumn. On the first night, my friends and I watched the moon rising from a boat on the lake. We ate mooncakes and drank Longjing tea. The next day we walked along the lake. It was such a nice trip.

去年秋天，我参观了西湖。第一晚，我和朋友们坐在湖上的船里，欣赏月亮升起。我们吃了月饼，喝了龙井茶。第二天，我们沿着湖边散步。这真是一次美好的旅行。

(Jessica, nurse)

（杰西卡，护士）
