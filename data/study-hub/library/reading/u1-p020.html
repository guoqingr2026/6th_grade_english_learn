🟠 A magical island

🟠 神奇的岛屿

On Easter Island, I saw huge statues called Moai. The Rapa Nui people made them thousands of years ago. I loved the food on the island. The locals cooked fresh food on rocks! We had fish. It was so delicious!

在复活节岛，我看到了巨大的雕像，叫做"摩艾"。拉帕努伊人在几千年前建造了它们。我非常喜欢岛上的食物。当地人在岩石上烹饪新鲜食物！我们吃了鱼，真是太美味了！

(Lucy, 10-year-old student)

（露西，10岁学生）

🟠 An unusual "table"

🟠 不寻常的"桌子"

I climbed many mountains on my trips, but Table Mountain in South Africa was a special one. Its name comes from its top. It is flat like a table! But I found it only looks flat from one side.

我在旅途中爬过很多山，但南非的桌山是一座特别的山。它的名字来源于它的山顶——像桌子一样平坦！但我发现它只从一面看起来是平的。

I went to the top by cable car. The first cable car opened in 1929. The view was so amazing!

我乘缆车到达山顶。第一条缆车于1929年开通，那里的景色真是太令人惊叹了！

(William, mountain lover)

（威廉，登山爱好者）
