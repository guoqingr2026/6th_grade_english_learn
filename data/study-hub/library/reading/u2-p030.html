✅ 参考答案示例

Present your snapshots

示例：

These are snapshots from our Double Ninth Festival event at school. Chen Jie and Liu Xin played the pipa to greet the old people. The old people loved it very much. Mike and John did wonderful kung fu. What a great show! We also made zongzi and sang songs together. Everyone had a great time!

这些是我们学校重阳节活动的精彩瞬间。陈洁和刘鑫弹奏琵琶来欢迎老人们。老人们非常喜欢。迈克和约翰表演了精彩的功夫，真是一场精彩的演出！我们还一起包了粽子，唱了歌。每个人都度过了美好的时光！
