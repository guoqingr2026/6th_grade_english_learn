【Read and write】 🎧

【读写练习】 🎧

❓ Look at the pictures. How do they feel?

❓ 看图片。他们感觉怎么样？

❓ Did you feel this way before? Why?

❓ 你以前有过这种感受吗？为什么？

【Emotion Icons】

【情绪图标】

😢 Sad 😢 伤心

😂 Laughing happily

😂 开心大笑

😟 Worried/Nervous

😟 烦恼/紧张

😒 Uncomfortable/Sick

😒 不舒服/生病

📌 Paragraph 1

📌 段落一

😢 Liu Jia was crying.

😢 刘佳在哭泣。

So Miss White asked her

于是怀特老师问她：

What's the matter, Liu Jia? You look sad.

怎么了，刘佳？你看起来很难过。

Liu Jia answered

刘佳回答道：

I'm feeling down.

我心情很低落。

My good friend Lucy moved to another city.

我的好朋友露西搬到了另一个城市去了。

📌 Paragraph 2

📌 段落二

Miss White listened carefully and told Liu Jia

怀特老师仔细地听完，然后告诉刘佳：

Think about happy things.

多想想开心的事。

Call your friend or send her some emails.

给你的朋友打个电话，或者给她发几封邮件。

📌 Paragraph 3

📌 段落三

I tried that, but I still feel unhappy, said Liu Jia.

我试过了，但我仍然感到不开心，刘佳说道。

📌 Paragraph 4

📌 段落四

Miss White gave her a glass of water.

怀特老师递给她一杯水。

Maybe you can do some exercise.

也许你可以做些运动。

Exercise may cheer you up!

运动也许能让你振作起来！

📌 Paragraph 5

📌 段落五

Join some after-school clubs and make new friends!

参加一些课外兴趣小组，结交新朋友！

You can talk and have fun with them, added Miss White.

你可以和他们交流，一起开心，怀特老师补充道。

📌 Paragraph 6

📌 段落六

I will try. Thank you, Miss White, said Liu Jia.

我会试试的。谢谢您，怀特老师，刘佳说。

✉️ Liu Jia's Reply Letter

✉️ 刘佳的回信

📮 Dear Miss White

📮 亲爱的怀特老师：

I often make video calls with my friend now.

我现在经常和我的朋友进行视频通话。

I also joined a ping-pong club and made some new friends.

我还加入了乒乓球俱乐部，结交了一些新朋友。

I feel much better.

我感觉好多了。

Thank you so much!

非常感谢您！

Liu Jia 😊

刘佳 😊
