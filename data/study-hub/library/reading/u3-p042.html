3️⃣ Healthy lifestyle pamphlet reference answers

3️⃣ 健康生活方式小册子参考答案

🏃 Take care of your body

🏃 照顾好你的身体

Don't stay up late.

不要熬夜。

Drink enough water every day.

每天喝足够的水。

Exercise often.

经常锻炼。

Eat healthy food.

吃健康的食物。

😊 Make yourself feel good

😊 让自己感觉良好

Think about happy things.

想想开心的事情。

Take a deep breath when you feel sad.

当你感到伤心时做一个深呼吸。

Talk to your friends or family.

和你的朋友或家人聊聊天。

Do things you enjoy.

做你喜欢的事情。

🤝 Be friendly with people around you

🤝 对你周围的人友好

Join clubs and teams.

加入社团和团队。

Help others when they need you.

在别人需要你的时候帮助他们。

Be a volunteer.

做一名志愿者。

Spend time with your family.

花时间陪伴你的家人。
