🖐️✋ 1. My name is Right Hand. This is my twin brother, Left Hand.

🖐️✋ 1. 我的名字叫右手（Right Hand）。这是我的双胞胎兄弟，左手（Left Hand）。

🧒🍎 2. One day, our master didn't wash us and ate his food. He felt ill the next day. Now he washes us before every meal.

🧒🍎 2. 有一天，我们的主人没有洗手就吃东西了。他第二天感觉不舒服。现在他每次吃饭前都会洗手。

🤧🧴 3. On another day, our master had a bad cold. He had a runny nose and a cough. We wiped his nose and covered his mouth with a tissue. We're important for his physical health.

🤧🧴 3. 又有一天，我们的主人得了重感冒。他流鼻涕还咳嗽。我们帮他擦鼻子，并用纸巾捂住他的嘴巴。我们对他的身体健康很重要。

🎨📚 4. We're also important for his mental and social health. One day, our master felt unhappy. But we helped him make handicrafts. He felt happy again.

🎨📚 4. 我们对他的心理健康和社交健康也很重要。有一天，我们的主人感到不开心。但我们帮助他制作了手工艺品。他又开心起来了。
