👩 Mum: How much pocket money do you have?

👧 Sarah: I have one hundred and forty-six yuan.

👧 莎拉：我有一百四十六元。

👩 Mum: What else are you going to buy?

👩 妈妈：你还打算买什么？

👧 Sarah: A lot. But first I'm going to buy a pair of sunglasses for Grandpa.

👧 莎拉：很多东西。但首先我打算给爷爷买一副太阳眼镜。

👩 Mum: Why?

👩 妈妈：为什么？

👧 Sarah: Because he lost his old pair last weekend.

👧 莎拉：因为他上周末把旧的那副弄丢了。

👩 Mum: That's so nice of you!

👩 妈妈：你真是太贴心了！

👧 Sarah: I also want to buy a microscope.

👧 莎拉：我还想买一台显微镜。

👩 Mum: Good idea! But microscopes are expensive.

👩 妈妈：好主意！但是显微镜很贵。

👧 Sarah: I also have some lucky money from the Spring Festival, but I still need to save more.

👧 莎拉：我还有一些春节压岁钱，但我仍然需要多存一些。

👩 Mum: Maybe we can wait for a sale!

👩 妈妈：也许我们可以等打折的时候再买！
