Mr Liu: Hi, children. Let's talk about our planet Earth today. What can you see in the sky?

刘老师：大家好。今天我们来谈谈我们的地球。你们在天空中能看到什么？

Mike: The sky is often blue in the daytime. We can see the Sun and the clouds. We can also see birds and planes.

迈克：白天天空通常是蓝色的。我们可以看到太阳和云朵，还可以看到鸟和飞机。

Amy: At night, we can see the Moon and the stars.

艾米：晚上，我们可以看到月亮和星星。

Mr Liu: Very good. But what can we see from outer space? Let's look at some photos from a space station.

刘老师：非常好。但从外太空我们能看到什么呢？让我们来看一些来自空间站的照片。

Binbin: Wow! We can see mountains, oceans and clouds. How wonderful!

彬彬：哇！我们可以看到山脉、海洋和云朵。真是太壮观了！

Chen Jie: There are large oceans on Earth. It looks like a big blue marble.

陈洁：地球上有广阔的海洋。它看起来像一颗巨大的蓝色弹珠。

Mr Liu: Right. Earth also has some beautiful names. For example, the Blue Planet and Mother Earth.

刘老师：对。地球还有一些美丽的名字，例如蓝色星球和地球母亲。

Mike: I like those names. I want to know more about our Mother Earth!

迈克：我喜欢这些名字。我想更多地了解我们的地球母亲！
