分享示例：

The Sun is very big and hot. There isn't any life on it. But it gives us light and keeps us warm.

太阳非常大而且炽热。上面没有任何生命。但它给我们光和温暖。

Earth goes around the Sun. So we have four seasons. Earth spins, so we have day and night. In the daytime, we can see planes, birds, ...

地球绕着太阳运转，所以我们有四个季节。地球自转，所以我们有白天和黑夜。白天，我们可以看到飞机、鸟……

An astronaut studies space, stars and planets. They work in ...

宇航员研究太空、星星和行星。他们在……工作。
