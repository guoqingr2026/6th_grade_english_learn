What makes some rivers and lakes dry up?

是什么让一些河流和湖泊干涸？

Do Binbin and Mike think it's too late to change? Why?

彬彬和迈克认为改变已经太迟了吗？为什么？

Mrs Green: We use energy every day. But many resources are running out. What should we do?

格林老师：我们每天都在使用能源。但许多资源正在耗尽。我们应该怎么做？

Mike: We should save energy. Then we will use fewer resources.

迈克：我们应该节约能源。这样我们就会使用更少的资源。

Mrs Green: How can we save energy?

格林老师：我们怎样才能节约能源？

Binbin: We should change our energy use.

彬彬：我们应该改变我们的能源使用方式。

Mrs Green: Can you give us an example, Binbin?

格林老师：彬彬，你能举个例子吗？

Binbin: Sure. We can take quick showers. That will save energy and water.

彬彬：当然。我们可以快速淋浴。这样可以节约能源和水。

Mike: I agree. We can also use LED lighting.

迈克：我同意。我们还可以使用节能灯。

Binbin: And we should always unplug our computers and TVs.

彬彬：我们还应该在不用时拔掉电脑和电视的插头。

Mrs Green: Great! Small changes can make a big difference.

格林老师：太好了！小小的改变可以带来巨大的不同。

Why do we have to save energy? We have to save energy because many resources are running out.

What did Binbin and Mike suggest? They suggested taking quick showers, using LED lighting and unplugging computers and TVs.
