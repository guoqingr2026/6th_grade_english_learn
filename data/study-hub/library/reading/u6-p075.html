What energy do you often use every day? Do people waste energy? How?

你每天经常使用哪种能源？人们会浪费能源吗？怎么浪费的？

Save energy, save our Earth!

节约能源，拯救地球！

Mother Earth is running out of resources! What should we do? Here are our top three ideas. We should all try to save energy and save our Earth!

地球母亲的资源正在耗尽！我们该怎么办？以下是我们最重要的三个建议。我们都应该努力节约能源，拯救我们的地球！

Idea 1

建议一

Useful tips to save water:

节约用水的实用建议：

Take a quick shower.

快速淋浴。

Wash all your dirty clothes together, not one at a time.

把所有脏衣服一起洗，而不是一件一件地洗。

Wash fruit and vegetables, then use the water again.

洗完水果和蔬菜后，把水再次利用。

Think: How does a quick shower save energy?

思考：快速淋浴如何节约能源？

Idea 2

建议二

Useful tips to use less electricity:

节约用电的实用建议：

Use LED lighting.

使用节能灯。

Turn off your TV or computer and unplug it.

关闭电视或电脑并拔掉插头。

Set your air conditioner to 26°C or more in summer.

夏天将空调温度设定在26摄氏度或以上。

Think: How can you use air conditioners less?

思考：你如何减少空调的使用？

Idea 3

建议三

Great tips to save and recycle resources:

节约和回收资源的好建议：

Use both sides of paper.

两面都使用纸张。

Use old bottles again. You can put noodles or pencils in them.

再次使用旧瓶子。你可以用它们来装面条或铅笔。

Think: What are your ideas for following the "3R" rules?

思考：你有什么遵循"3R"原则的好想法？
