✅ 参考答案示例

Choose the best title for each idea:

Idea 1 ( A ) — Do small things to save water.

Idea 2 ( C ) — Save electricity.

Idea 3 ( B ) — Reduce, reuse and recycle.

Think: How does a quick shower save energy? A quick shower uses less hot water. Heating water needs energy, so using less water saves more energy.

Think: How can you use air conditioners less? We can open windows to let in fresh air. We can also use fans instead of air conditioners.

Think: What are your ideas for following the "3R" rules? We can reuse old bottles as pencil holders. We can recycle old newspapers and books. We can bring our own bags when we go shopping.

回信示例：

Dear Editor,

We are running out of resources. But it is not too late. We can do things to change this. Here are my ideas.

Tips to save energy and resources:

Take quick showers and wash clothes together to save water.

Use LED lighting and unplug computers and TVs to save electricity.

Use both sides of paper and recycle old bottles, clothes and books.

Sincerely,

Li Ming
