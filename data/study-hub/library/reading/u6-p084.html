🏫 Welcome! 🏫

欢迎！

Did you enjoy listening to the bird song? Did you feel the cool wind from the sea? Is your stress gone?

你喜欢听鸟鸣声吗？你感受到了来自大海的凉风吗？你的压力消除了吗？

Stress is sometimes bad for us. Science can help, but we also need to help ourselves. What can we do? Well, we should take care of our body. We need to have a healthy diet and exercise too. Remember, get enough sleep and don't stay up late.

压力有时对我们有害。科学可以帮助我们，但我们也需要自助。我们能做什么呢？好吧，我们应该照顾好自己的身体。我们需要健康饮食，也需要锻炼。记住，要保证充足的睡眠，不要熬夜。

We must also take care of our mind. Think about happy things. You can keep a diary, join some clubs, listen to music or read books. Spend time with your friends and family. Give them a big smile. Go out and have some fun!

我们还必须照顾好自己的心理。想想快乐的事情。你可以写日记、参加一些俱乐部、听音乐或读书。花时间陪伴你的朋友和家人。给他们一个大大的微笑。出去玩，开开心！

I hope this area can help you!

我希望这个区域能帮助到你！
