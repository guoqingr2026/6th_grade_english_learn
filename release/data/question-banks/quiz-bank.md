## Unit 1

### u1a-1 · Unit 1 Part A 旅行闯关
- **题干**：选择正确句子：你上周六做了什么？
- **选项**：What do you do last Saturday? / What did you do last Saturday? / What did you did last Saturday? / What were you do last Saturday?
- **答案**：What did you do last Saturday?
- **解析**：过去时提问结构是 What did + 主语 + 动词原形。

### u1b-1 · Unit 1 Part B 旅行闯关

- **选项**：go / went / gone / going
