课文填空与问答精讲

  1. I am going to save half of it in my piggy bank.
    save half = 存一半。
  2. I shouldn't waste money on things I don't need.
    shouldn't + 动词原形。
  3. Write a spending plan: 列出 need 与 want 各 2 项。
    区分必需品与想要的物品。

重要写作练习讲解 · 理财短文（满分范文）

How I Manage My Pocket Money

I get some pocket money every month. I am going to save half of it. I want to save up for a new basketball. I also want to buy books on sale. I shouldn't waste money on things I don't need. Managing money well is important!

得分要点：一般现在时 + be going to + want/need + shouldn't。
