课文填空与问答精讲

    comes — 主语 it 三单。
  2. 用 should 写一句环保建议：___
    We should turn off lights when we leave.
  3. green energy 包括哪些？
    solar power, wind power, water power

语法仿写 · Where does...come from?

Where does wind power come from? It comes from the wind. Where does solar power come from? It comes from the sun. Green energy helps protect the Earth.
