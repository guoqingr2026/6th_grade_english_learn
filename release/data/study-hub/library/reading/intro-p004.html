致同学

Dear Students

随着新学期的开始，你们将进入小学阶段的最后一年。在这本书里，你们将和老朋友们一起继续了解丰富多彩的中外文化，学习独立思考和多角度地看待事物。

As the new semester begins, you are entering the final year of primary school. In this book, you will continue to explore the rich and diverse cultures of China and the world with your old friends, and learn to think independently and see things from multiple perspectives.

在每单元的必学板块Part A和Part B里，你们将学习中外地标性建筑和节假日活动，知道零用钱管理的小窍门，初步了解宇宙和能源知识。在选学板块Part C里还会有有趣的项目活动和拓展阅读。每个单元的起始页都提出了一个与你的学习、生活相关的问题，希望你们能结合自己的知识、经验和感受，认真思考问题的答案。

In the compulsory sections Part A and Part B of each unit, you will learn about famous landmarks in China and abroad, holiday activities, tips for managing pocket money, and basic knowledge about the universe and energy. The optional section Part C includes interesting project activities and extended reading. At the beginning of each unit, there is a question related to your studies and life. We hope you will think carefully about the answers, drawing on your own knowledge, experience and feelings.

这本书为你们准备了许多有趣的对话和丰富的语言活动，助你们学会灵活运用所学语言进行交流，表达自己的观点，能读懂不同类型的英语文章，还能学到有用的语法知识，体验到文化的多样性。

This book has prepared many interesting dialogues and rich language activities for you, helping you learn to use the language flexibly in communication, express your own opinions, read different types of English texts, learn useful grammar knowledge, and experience cultural diversity.

越学好奇心，一定要多听、多说、多读、多看、多写。你们上课最好认真听讲，学习遇到困难时积极与他人探讨，寻求帮助，还要在学习中认真反思，与他人交流，做到反思自己的学习情况，努力找到适合自己的学习方法，养成良好的学习习惯。

The more you learn, the more curious you should be. Remember to listen more, speak more, read more, observe more, and write more. In class, try your best to listen carefully. When you face difficulties, actively discuss with others and seek help. Also remember to reflect on your learning, exchange ideas with others, find study methods that suit you, and develop good study habits.

愿你们和书里的小主人公们一起享受小学生活的美好时光。

May you enjoy the wonderful time of primary school life together with the little characters in this book.
