1. What did you learn about the Jinggang Mountains? The Jinggang Mountains are a UNESCO World Cultural Heritage Site. There is a Jinggangshan Revolution Museum with old books and photos about the Red Army. You can climb the mountains, see bamboo forests and waterfalls, and eat delicious pumpkin soup and red rice. It is only 1.5 hours from the airport by bus.

井冈山是联合国教科文组织世界文化遗产。那里有一座井冈山革命博物馆，陈列着关于红军的旧书籍和照片。你可以攀登山峰，欣赏竹林和瀑布，还能品尝美味的南瓜汤和红米饭。从机场乘车只需约1.5小时。

2. What does the word "must-see" mean in the first review? "Must-see" means that the place is so special and interesting that everyone should visit it. It is a place you cannot miss.

"Must-see"

的意思是这个地方非常特别、有趣，每个人都应该去参观。这是一个不容错过的地方。

3. Do you want to go to the Jinggang Mountains after reading the reviews? Why? Yes, I do. I want to go there because the views are wonderful and the food is delicious. I also want to learn more about the history of the Red Army.

是的，我想去。因为那里的风景很壮观，食物也很美味。我还想更多地了解红军的历史。
