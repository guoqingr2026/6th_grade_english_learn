张鹏：我去广州旅行了。

John: What did you do?

约翰：你做了什么？

Zhang Peng: ...

张鹏：……

约翰：我去了杭州和绍兴，我去参观了鲁迅的故居。他是一位著名的作家。

Zhang Peng: Yes, he was. He wrote a lot of great books.

Amy: ... 艾米：……

说明文字）：

Guangzhou

广州： I took a trip to Guangzhou and visited the Guangdong Science Centre. It was very interesting! 我去广州旅行，参观了广东科学中心，非常有趣！

Shaoxing

绍兴： I went to Shaoxing and visited the former home of Lu Xun. He was a famous writer and wrote many great books. 我去了绍兴，参观了鲁迅的故居。他是一位著名的作家，写了很多伟大的著作。

Mount Huangshan

黄山： I climbed Mount Huangshan and watched the beautiful sunrise. The view was amazing! 我爬了黄山，观看了美丽的日出，景色真是太壮观了！

Zhang Peng（

补充）： I visited the Guangdong Science Centre. I saw many interesting science exhibits. It was amazing! 我参观了广东科学中心，看到了很多有趣的科学展品，真是太棒了！

Amy（ 补充）： I climbed Mount Huangshan. The view from the top was wonderful. I took many beautiful photos there. 我爬了黄山，山顶的景色非常壮观，我在那里拍了很多漂亮的照片。
