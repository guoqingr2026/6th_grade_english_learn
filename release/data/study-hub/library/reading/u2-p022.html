思维导图示例：

Spring Festival

春节

Where and when? In China, in January or February.

在哪里？什么时候？在中国，一月或二月。

What do people see and hear? Fireworks, lion dances and music.

人们看到和听到什么？烟花、舞狮和音乐。

What do people do? Eat jiaozi, paste fu, visit relatives.

人们做什么？吃饺子、贴福字、走亲访友。

How do people feel? Happy and excited.

人们有什么感受？快乐又兴奋。
