Scene 3

第三幕

Narrator: Nian was very afraid and ran back to the sea. The next day, the villagers came back to the village.

旁白："年"非常害怕，跑回了大海。第二天，村民们回到了村子里。

Villager 1: I can't believe it! All our animals are safe!

村民一：真不敢相信！我们所有的动物都平安无事！

Villager 2: How did you do it?

村民二：你是怎么做到的？

Old man: Last night, I pasted red paper on the doors and windows and I also lit many candles. Finally, I set off fireworks. He ran away!

老人：昨晚，我在门窗上贴了红纸，还点了许多蜡烛。最后，我放了烟花。它就跑走了！

Villagers: Hooray! We are safe!

村民们：万岁！我们得救了！

Narrator: From then on, on New Year's Eve, people get together to celebrate the coming new year. They paste fu and Spring Festival couplets on their doors and set off fireworks. This is why the Spring Festival is also called guonian.

旁白：从那以后，每逢除夕，人们便聚在一起庆祝新年的到来。他们在门上贴福字和春联，并燃放烟花。这就是为什么春节也被称为"过年"的原因。

✅ 参考答案示例

Why is the Spring Festival also called guonian? Because from then on, people get together on New Year's Eve to celebrate the coming new year. They paste fu and Spring Festival couplets on their doors and set off fireworks, just like the old man did to scare Nian away.

春节为什么也叫"过年"？ 因为从那时起，人们每逢除夕便聚在一起庆祝新年。他们在门上贴福字和春联，燃放烟花，就像那位老人赶走"年"时所做的那样。

What did the old man do to scare Nian away? He pasted red paper on the doors and windows, lit many candles and set off fireworks. Nian hated the colour red, bright lights and loud noises, so it ran away.

那位老人做了什么来吓走"年"？ 他在门窗上贴了红纸，点了许多蜡烛，还燃放了烟花。"年"讨厌红色、强光和巨大的声响，所以它就逃走了。
