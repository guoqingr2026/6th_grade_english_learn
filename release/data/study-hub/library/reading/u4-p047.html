🟡 Let's talk

🟡 让我们来谈一谈

Mum is giving Matt his pocket money. Listen and write T (True) or F (False). 🎧

妈妈正在给马特零花钱。听录音，写T或F。🎧

Matt gets 25 yuan for his piggy bank. （ ）

马特得到了25元放入存钱罐。（ ）

Matt is going to buy some pencils. （ ）

马特打算买一些铅笔。（ ）

👦 Mike: Are you counting your pocket money, Matt?

👦 迈克：马特，你在数你的零花钱吗？

👦 Matt: Yes. I have three hundred and fifty-seven yuan.

👦 马特：是的。我有三百五十七元。

👦 Mike: Wow, that's a lot. How are you going to spend it?

👦 迈克：哇，好多啊。你打算怎么花这些钱？

👦 Matt: I'm going to buy a schoolbag.

👦 马特：我打算买一个书包。

👦 Mike: Yes, you need a new one.

👦 迈克：是的，你需要一个新的。

👦 Matt: I also want to buy some books.

👦 马特：我还想买一些书。

👦 Mike: Nice. Maybe you can buy some animal books. They're interesting.

👦 迈克：不错。也许你可以买一些动物类的书，很有趣的。

👦 Matt: Great idea! I like animals. Should I also buy some ice cream?

👦 马特：好主意！我喜欢动物。我也应该买一些冰淇淋吗？

👦 Mike: Sure, but don't eat too much.

👦 迈克：当然可以，但是不要吃太多。

👦 Matt: You're right. I won't.

👦 马特：你说得对。我不会的。
