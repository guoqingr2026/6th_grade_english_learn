1️⃣ A long time ago, people exchanged goods or services. Sometimes it was difficult to get the things they wanted.

1️⃣ 很久以前，人们通过交换商品或服务来进行贸易。有时候很难得到他们想要的东西。

2️⃣ Later, people used different forms of money. But what did the money look like? Look! There is shell money, knife money, copper coins, ...

2️⃣ 后来，人们使用了不同形式的货币。但是这些货币长什么样呢？看！有贝壳货币、刀币、铜钱……

3️⃣ metal coins 金属货币 50 kilos of coins = a roll of silk

3️⃣ 金属货币 50公斤的金属货币 = 一匹丝绸

But metal coins were heavy to carry. So people used paper money.

但是金属货币携带起来很重。所以人们开始使用纸币。

4️⃣ We still use paper money today. We also have bankbooks and cards.

4️⃣ 我们今天仍然在使用纸币。我们还有存折和银行卡。
