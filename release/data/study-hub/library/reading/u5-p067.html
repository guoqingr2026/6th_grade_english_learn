①

The closest planet to the Sun is not the hottest.

离太阳最近的行星并不是最热的。

The hottest planet is Venus. It has a temperature of about 467°C.

最热的行星是金星。它的温度约为467摄氏度。

②

Jupiter's storm may be more than 300 years old.

木星上的风暴可能已经持续了300多年。

The name of the big storm on Jupiter is the Great Red Spot. It is wider than Earth.

木星上这场大风暴的名字叫大红斑。它比地球还宽。

③

Neptune has the fastest wind in the solar system.

海王星拥有太阳系中最快的风。

Neptune's winds can reach over 2,000 kilometres per hour.

海王星的风速可以超过每小时2000千米。

Is there any water on these planets?

这些行星上有水吗？

Very little.

非常少。

So there are no fish out there.

所以那里没有鱼。
