✅ 参考答案示例

How does the box keep the heat? The box keeps the heat by trapping sunlight inside. The black paper absorbs more heat from the sun.

Why do we use black paper? We use black paper because black absorbs more sunlight and heat than other colors.

What did Binbin and Robin learn from this experience? They learned that sunlight is a great source of energy. We can use solar power to cook food, light our homes and run cars. We should use energy in smart ways.

彬彬和罗宾从这次经历中学到了什么？ 他们了解到阳光是一种很好的能源。我们可以利用太阳能来做饭、为房屋提供照明以及驱动汽车。我们应该聪明地使用能源。
